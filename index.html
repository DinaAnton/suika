SUIKA

Drop the little animals, merge them, and grow the biggest one! 🐣→🐋

How to play

  • 🖱️ / 👆 Move the animal left and right, then click / tap to drop it.
  • ⌨️ Or use the ← → arrow keys and Space / Enter to drop.
  • 🔗 Two identical animals that touch merge into a bigger one!
  • 🚨 If the pile rises above the red line and stays there, the game is over.
  • 🏆 Chain quick merges into combos for bonus points!
  • 🐋 / 🐼 / 🐻‍❄️ The biggest animal in each world is the "final boss".
